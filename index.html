A note from our CEO

Okay, fine. Hi, I'm Eeman.

The Bouquets started with flowers, and I still love them. But somewhere along the way I realised a bouquet was never really about the flowers — it's about the second someone opens the door and sees it.

So I redesigned The Bouquets around that second. New wrapping, new shapes, and one simple rule: if the person you love would light up over it — their favourite serum, the snacks they hide from everyone, the little things that are so them — we can gather it into a bouquet.

And honestly? My favourite part is still the delivery. Every bouquet leaves us the way I'd want to receive one: carefully arranged and properly wrapped. Your reactions, and the messages you send us afterwards, are the best part of my job.

Thank you for letting us be part of your moments. Keep them coming — I'll keep delivering.

Eeman Dar

CEO, The Bouquets
